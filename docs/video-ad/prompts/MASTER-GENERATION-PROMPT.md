# Master AI Video Generation Prompt
## NyumbaSearch — “FIND. CONNECT. MOVE IN.” (90–120s)

Create a premium, cinematic, emotionally engaging advertisement for **NyumbaSearch**, a Kenyan housing technology platform focused on making house hunting, property management, tenant experiences, service-provider connections and housing-related financial-service connections easier.

The advertisement must feel **human-made, authentic and professionally produced**, not like an obvious AI-generated commercial.

**Setting:** Modern Nairobi, Kenya — recognizable but natural urban environments, realistic apartment buildings, residential neighborhoods, roads, pedestrians, vehicles, property managers, landlords, tenants, house hunters and local service providers.

**Story arc:**  
Frustration of house hunting (outdated info, endless calls, unanswered messages, unverified listings, wasted transport, unavailable properties, hard comparisons, trust uncertainty) → NyumbaSearch as the calmer solution → multi-audience value (hunters, tenants, owners/managers, service providers, careful finance referrals) → emotional payoff of moving in → brand line **FIND. CONNECT. MOVE IN.** → CTA to **nyumbasearch.com** (and Google Play only if the listing is publicly live).

**UI fidelity:** Use the supplied NyumbaSearch screenshots as visual references for the actual app experience, branding, logo, colors, interface structure, property cards, search, map and dashboards. Preserve the recognizable NyumbaSearch visual identity. Do not invent an unrelated app design.

**Audiences to show clearly**
- House hunters: search, compare, map, connect with responsible parties  
- Tenants: housing journey + home services + finance connections  
- Owners / landlords / managers: list, manage units/tenants, track rent/leases/invoices, reach tenants  
- Service providers: moving, cleaning, maintenance, plumbing, electrical and other approved home services  
- Financial services: **referral/connection pathway only** — no fake loan approvals, guaranteed financing, or invented interest rates  

**Transitions:** Real-life problems visually transform into simple digital solutions (calls → search; wasted travel → map; uncertain listing → verified experience; notebooks → dashboard; mover need → services; budget worry → finance interest form).

**Avoid:** exaggerated acting, futuristic holograms, robotic people, overly perfect environments, fake-looking UI, excessive transitions, neon colors, corporate stock-video aesthetics, aggressive selling.

**Look:** Warm natural lighting; cream, brown, subtle gold; cinematic establishing shots; intimate handheld smartphone shots; slow push-ins; smooth tracking; over-the-shoulder app interactions; elegant UI transitions.

**Audio:** Subtle African-inspired contemporary instrumental. Voiceover: calm, warm, reassuring, confident Kenyan/African English — empathetic, trustworthy, conversational.

**Pacing:** Slightly tense in the problem section → calmer when NyumbaSearch appears → uplifting toward the conclusion.

**End card:** Premium full-screen NyumbaSearch brand card, official logo clear.  
**FIND. CONNECT. MOVE IN.**  
**Visit: nyumbasearch.com**  
(Optional Play badge only when store listing is public.)

**Central idea:**  
NyumbaSearch isn't just helping people find houses. It's making the entire housing journey easier — from finding a property, to managing it, to living in it, to finding the services and financial connections people need around it.

Generate as a continuous 90–120 second piece, or as 13 scene clips for editorial assembly using the per-scene prompts in this folder.
