## SCENE 1 — THE REALITY OF HOUSE HUNTING (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** Early morning Nairobi. Young professional leaves apartment with phone and backpack. Walking streets, calling numbers, “Already taken,” looking at apartment from outside, WhatsApp unanswered, scrolling posts, suspicious listing, traffic, exhaustion. Natural lighting.
**Text:** HOUSE HUNTING IN NAIROBI? → IT SHOULDN'T BE THIS HARD.
**VO:** Finding a place to call home should be exciting. But for many people in Nairobi, house hunting means endless calls, outdated listings, wasted trips, unclear information… and sometimes, listings you simply can't trust.

## SCENE 2 — THE PROBLEMS (0:10–0:22)
**Visual:** Fast smooth montage — no answer, cross-town trip, occupied house, wallet after transport, multi-platform scrolling.
**Text:** OUTDATED LISTINGS · WASTED TIME · WASTED TRANSPORT MONEY · FAKE / UNVERIFIED LISTINGS · NO EASY WAY TO COMPARE
**VO:** You can spend hours searching, money moving around, and still not know whether the property is actually available — or who you're dealing with.

## SCENE 3 — INTRODUCE NYUMBASEARCH (0:22–0:32)
**Visual:** Pace slows. Phone focuses. Open NyumbaSearch using real screenshots (logo, search, cards, map, filters, details).
**Text:** MEET NYUMBASEARCH · A SMARTER WAY TO FIND HOME.
**VO:** That’s why we built NyumbaSearch. A smarter way to discover properties, connect with the right people, and make the journey from searching to moving in much easier.

## SCENE 4 — SMART PROPERTY SEARCH (0:32–0:45)
**Visual:** Search “1 Bedroom — Nairobi”, filters, map zoom, markers, property tap, images/details/contact.
**Text:** SEARCH · COMPARE · DISCOVER · CONNECT
**VO:** Instead of jumping from post to post, search in one place. Explore properties by location, price, and what actually matters to you. See property details, compare options, view locations on the map, and connect directly with the people responsible for the property.

## SCENE 5 — TRUST & VERIFICATION (0:45–0:55)
**Visual:** Owner + NyumbaSearch rep; submit → contact → check → approve → verified in app; tenant views confidently.
**Text:** TRUST MATTERS. · VERIFIED PROPERTIES
**VO:** And because trust matters, NyumbaSearch works to validate properties before they become part of the platform. We don't just open the door to anyone claiming to have a property.

## SCENE 6 — PROPERTY OWNERS & LANDLORDS (0:55–1:08)
**Visual:** Owner dashboard — portfolio, units, vacant unit listed, tenant discovers it.
**Text:** LIST · MANAGE · TRACK · GROW
**VO:** For property owners and managers, NyumbaSearch isn't just about listing a vacant unit. It helps you manage your properties, organize units and tenants, track rent and payment records, keep your property information organized, and connect with potential tenants.

## SCENE 7 — PROPERTY MANAGEMENT (1:08–1:20)
**Visual:** Manager dashboard; rent payment notification; collection; invoices; leases; units. Subtle animation.
**Text:** PROPERTY MANAGEMENT · RENT COLLECTION · TENANT MANAGEMENT · ORGANIZED RECORDS
**VO:** From leases and units to rent collection, invoices, and tenant activity, property management becomes more organized and easier to follow.

## SCENE 8 — SERVICE PROVIDERS (1:20–1:31)
**Visual:** Move-in needs (mover, cleaning, plumbing, electrical); services section; trusted providers; request received.
**Text:** FIND A HOME. · FIND THE HELP YOU NEED.
**VO:** Moving into a home also comes with everything around the home. That’s where NyumbaSearch connects tenants and property owners with trusted service providers — from moving and maintenance to other essential home services.
