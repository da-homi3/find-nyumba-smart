1. Executive overview

NyumbaSearch operates a live digital housing ecosystem in Kenya — property discovery with verification controls, direct contact pathways, home-service discovery, and property-management entry points. Partners can meet customers at high-intent moments: searching, verifying, moving and managing a home.

Thesis for partnersHousing intent is recurring and high-stakes. Distribution next to that intent can be more efficient than generic digital acquisition — when trust and consent are respected.

2. About NyumbaSearch

Built in Nairobi for Kenyan renters, landlords, managers and service providers. Live product: nyumbasearch.com. Co-founders: Faith Wanjiku (Product) and Kevin Buluma (Engineering).

3. The market problem

Housing search and operations remain fragmented. Trust is uneven. Services, finance and insurance are usually discovered after the stressful move — not during it. Institutions struggle to reach verified housing-intent customers without expensive, low-context campaigns.

4. Our solution

An integrated journey: discover → verify → contact → move → manage, with services and referral surfaces attached at the right step.

6–8. Partnership opportunity, benefits & models

Model A — Distribution

Partner promotes NyumbaSearch to customers; NyumbaSearch surfaces partner offers at housing moments. Commercial: CPA / revenue share / sponsored placement.

Model B — Referral

Structured lead forms (finance eligibility, insurance quotes). Minimal necessary fields. Consent-based handoff. Commission per qualified lead or bind.

Model C — Technology integration

Payments, KYC, messaging, data exchange APIs — only after legal/security review. No claim of existing regulated integrations unless contracted.

Model D — Strategic investment

Capital plus distribution. Terms upon discussion. Board/observer rights as negotiated.

Model E — Co-marketing

Campus, employer, branch and digital campaigns with shared creative and tracked URLs.

Model F — Ecosystem

Multi-product collaboration across listings, PM, services, finance and insurance for a defined customer segment (e.g. students, SME landlords).

10–13. Integration, marketing, data & commercial

  • Technology: tracked landing pages first; APIs later under DPA.
  • Marketing: in-product modules, email (opt-in), co-branded campaigns.
  • Data & insights: aggregated, privacy-respecting housing-intent insights — not raw PII dumps.
  • Commercial: placement fees, subscription sponsorships, CPA, revenue share, or equity-linked strategic deals.

14. Pilot program

Suggested 90-day pilot

  1. Define segment + offer (e.g. tenant contents insurance or mortgage pre-check).
  2. Launch consent-based form + tracked CTA on relevant product surfaces.
  3. Weekly lead quality review; iterate fields and messaging.
  4. Decide scale / pause based on agreed metrics.

15. Success metrics

Lead volume, qualified rate, contactability, conversion to product, partner CAC vs baseline, customer complaints / consent issues, incremental NyumbaSearch engagement. Exact targets set per pilot — not invented here.
