NyumbaSearch02 / Executive summary

The problem

Finding a home is still fragmented, costly and risky.

1 · Fragmented discovery

Listings scattered across agents, social groups and classifieds with inconsistent data.

2 · Expensive trust

Renters waste time and money on inaccurate, stale or unavailable listings.

3 · Journey ends too early

Most products stop at the listing — not tenancy, rent, or maintenance.

4 · Owners lack modern tools

Small landlords and managers need better lead capture, workflows and visibility.

The opportunity Own the user relationship before, during and after the move.

"Is it still available?" · "Can I see it today?" · "Never replies…"

Common renter pain points observed in Nairobi housing search — informal channels, low response rates, weak verification.

Business model

Four revenue layers — recurring at the core.

1 · Landlord & listing plans

KES 999 – 4,999 / month — visibility, lead management, premium placement. Free tier for onboarding.

2 · Property management SaaS

KES 2,000 – 10,000 / month per portfolio — rent collection, invoices, tenant workflows, dashboards.

3 · Service provider monetization

KES 1,500 – 5,000 / month — directory subscriptions, qualified leads, category sponsorships.

4 · Financial & protection referrals

Revenue share on mortgages, insurance, loans, and rent-protection products at housing intent moments.

Estimated annual revenue at full sector activation Estimate USD 2.5M – 4.5M+ when listing SaaS, PM tools, services marketplace, and financial referrals are all active across target markets.
