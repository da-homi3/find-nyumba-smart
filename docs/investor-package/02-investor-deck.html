Confidential01 / Cover

The housing problem

Finding a home is still unnecessarily difficult.

In Nairobi and across urban Kenya, housing search still runs through fragmented chats, informal intermediaries and listings that are hard to verify — before anyone even moves in or manages the home.

Search→ Verify→ Contact→ Move→ Manage

Where the journey breaks

  • Property information scattered across social groups and agents
  • Unreliable or stale vacancy claims
  • Weak trust signals before deposits and site visits
  • Time-consuming house hunting with limited transparency
  • Housing services discovered separately, after the fact
  • Landlords and managers operating with thin digital tooling

What tenants and owners feel

Tenants need confidence that a home is real, vacant and fairly presented. Owners need qualified demand without drowning in noise. Managers need operational continuity after the lease starts. Today those needs sit in different places — or nowhere digital at all.

No fabricated scam-rate statistics. Pain described from product positioning and observed market practice.

Trust & verification

Trust is part of the product.

NyumbaSearch does not allow random users to freely open listing or service-provider supply accounts without review. Applications are approved before listing access.

Listing submitted↓ Account review↓ Owner / responsible party contacted↓ Property validated↓ Listing published

Platform controls

  • Landlord / manager / agency onboarding with operations approval
  • Staged landlord verification states (phone → ID → business → ownership)
  • On-listing badges such as Verified / Business Verified
  • Optional paid site inspection tiers on /verify
  • Tenant-facing scam-risk messaging via Plus
