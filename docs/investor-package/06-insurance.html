Distribute protection at the moment of moving in

Tenant contents, landlord property cover and related products — via consented referral, not implied binding authority.

Opportunity

When a tenant finds a home — or a landlord onboards units — insurance suddenly becomes relevant. NyumbaSearch already surfaces an insurance quote pathway and states that referral commission may apply without changing the customer’s premium.

https://nyumbasearch.com/insurance · Live product surface

Product fit

Tenant contents

Theft, fire, water damage — timed to move-in.

Landlord property

Building and rental income protection for owners/managers.

Adjacent covers

Rent-related protection, service-provider liability — design with underwriting.

Referral form principles

Collect only what the partner needs to follow up:

  • Full name
  • Phone (and optional email)
  • Role (tenant / landlord / manager)
  • Product interest
  • Location / property context (high level)
  • Explicit consent to be contacted

No unnecessary sensitive data. Partner follows regulated advice / underwriting processes.

Commercial models

  • Cost per qualified lead
  • Commission on bind (as disclosed to customers)
  • Sponsored education modules
  • Later: embedded journeys subject to licensing and product approval

Important Embedded insurance requires confirmed regulatory pathway — not claimed as live today.

90-day pilot

  1. Pick one SKU (e.g. tenant contents from KES 500/mo messaging already on-site as product framing).
  2. Align quote SLA and brand disclosure.
  3. Measure lead→contact→quote→bind.
  4. Expand to landlord cover if quality holds.
