Digital Infrastructure for the African Housing Journey

NyumbaSearch is building a digital housing ecosystem designed to make property discovery, verification, management and housing-related services more accessible, trusted and efficient.

Confidential · August 2026
One-page briefing

1. Company

NyumbaSearch is a Kenyan housing technology platform connecting tenants with verified property owners, landlords, property managers and home-service providers. The product spans discovery, trust, direct contact, post-move services, and property operations — positioned as an emerging digital housing ecosystem, not a classifieds site alone.

2. Problem

Finding and securing housing in Nairobi still depends on fragmented WhatsApp groups, informal agents, and listings that are hard to trust. Tenants waste time verifying vacancy and legitimacy. Landlords and managers lack coherent digital tools for visibility, tenant acquisition and day-to-day operations. Housing-adjacent services remain disconnected from the move-in moment.

3. Solution

One platform for the housing journey: map-first discovery, staged verification, direct owner contact, neighbourhood intelligence (water, security, internet, commute), service-provider discovery, and property-management tooling for operators who need recurring operational infrastructure.

4. Product

  • Tenant: search, map, property detail, saved flows, Plus scam-risk signals, AI assistant
  • Supply: landlord / agency / manager portals with approval-gated listing access
  • Trust: verification badges, paid site inspection options, business verification states
  • Ecosystem: 24 home-service categories, finance & insurance referral surfaces, M-Pesa checkout messaging

5. Traction

Early-stage supply-side network with publicly observable inventory and services density. Historical growth chart not disclosed.

6. Business model

Published monetization surfaces: landlord/manager/agency subscriptions, listing boosts, NyumbaSearch Plus, contact unlocks, paid verification, service-provider tiers, advertising packages, finance/insurance referral pathways, property-management module billing. Current realized revenue is not publicly disclosed.

7. Market opportunity

Kenya’s real-estate sector remains a material share of GDP amid rapid urbanization and a persistent housing supply gap. Discovery and property operations are still largely offline or fragmented — creating room for trusted digital infrastructure focused first on Nairobi rental demand. External market data See deck for cited sources and illustrative TAM/SAM/SOM methodology.

8. Competitive advantage

  • Combination of discovery + verification workflow + direct contact + services + PM tooling
  • Approval-controlled supply accounts (not open self-serve listing spam)
  • Local product design: M-Pesa, neighbourhood signals, Kenyan service categories
  • Live product depth already spanning tenant, landlord, manager and services surfaces
