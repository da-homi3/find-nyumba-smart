NyumbaSearch as a housing-intent distribution channel

Positioned for commercial banks, microfinance, SACCOs and payment partners — without implying existing regulatory approvals or exclusive integrations.

One-sentence pitch NyumbaSearch concentrates verified housing intent — tenants searching, owners listing, managers operating — creating a consented pathway for relevant financial products.

Why banks should care

Housing moments predict near-term financial needs: deposits, furniture, school-term moves, MSME landlord cashflow, mortgages and construction. Most digital ads reach people without that context. NyumbaSearch meets them inside the journey.
