## Full narration (VO-MASTER)

### Chapter 1 — Welcome (0:00)

Welcome to NyumbaSearch for Property Managers.

In the next few minutes, you'll see how to get approved, open your dashboard, upload listings for the marketplace, and manage client portfolios — all from one place.

Whether you manage five units or five hundred, this walkthrough shows the exact screens you'll use every day.

### Chapter 2 — Getting started (0:25)

Start at **nyumbasearch.com/manager**.

This is the Property Manager Portal — built for firms that onboard landlord clients, publish listings, and route tenant leads.

Tap **Create manager account**. Enter your organization name and contact details.

NyumbaSearch operations reviews every application before dashboard access is granted. Once approved, sign in and you'll land on your dashboard.

Your first paid month unlocks the portal; your **second month is free** on manager plans.

### Chapter 3 — Dashboard overview (1:05)

This is your command centre.

At the top: **Total Properties**, **Property Views**, **Tenant Leads**, and **Monthly Revenue** — a quick pulse on portfolio performance.

The **Tools** row gives you bulk CSV import, API integrations, plan limits, and billing — the same power landlords get, tuned for multi-client work.

**Your properties** lists active marketplace listings with status, views, and quick actions.

On the right, **Viewing Requests** shows tenants who want to visit — confirm or decline in one click.

Use **Add property** any time to publish a new listing.

### Chapter 4 — Navigation (2:20)

The left sidebar is your map.

**Dashboard** — overview and KPIs.  
**Properties** — all marketplace listings.  
**Tenants & rent** — the Property Management module: units, leases, invoices, and M-Pesa rent collection.  
**Bulk import** — upload a CSV to create draft listings at once.  
**API & integrations** — connect external systems.  
**Messages** — tenant inquiries tied to each listing.  
**Caretakers** — on-site staff with PIN access.  
**Analytics** — views and lead performance.  
**Team** — invite staff with role-based access.  
**Plan** and **Billing** — subscriptions, lead packs, and payment history.  
**Rent payouts** — receive collected rent to bank or M-Pesa.

### Chapter 5 — Upload: Details (3:10)

Let's add a listing. Open **Properties → Add property**.

The wizard has four steps. First: **Details**.

Choose the property type — apartment, house, bedsitter, commercial, and more.

Add bedrooms, bathrooms, rent amount, and pricing mode — monthly, nightly, or sale.

Write a clear title and description. Select amenities tenants care about — parking, water, security, fibre.

Add contact name and phone numbers — tenants reach you directly through NyumbaSearch.

### Chapter 6 — Upload: Photos & media (4:05)

Step two: **Photos and media**.

Drag in high-quality photos — bright, honest, and well-lit. The platform enhances uploads for faster loading.

Add a walkthrough video or 360° tour if you have one. You can also paste a YouTube or Matterport link.

More media means more trust — and more viewing requests.

### Chapter 7 — Upload: Map pin (4:55)

Step three: **Map pin**.

Drop the pin on the exact building location. Tenants use map search — accurate pins get found faster.

Pick the county and neighbourhood from Kenya location lists so filters work correctly.

### Chapter 8 — Upload: Review (5:40)

Final step: **Review**.

Check every field, preview how the card will look, and run the listing quality check.

When you're satisfied, submit. New listings may go through verification before going live — that's how NyumbaSearch keeps the marketplace trustworthy.

You can edit, boost visibility, or deactivate any time from **Properties**.

### Chapter 9 — Tenants & rent (6:25)

Marketplace listings attract tenants. **Tenants & rent** is where you operate portfolios.

Subscribe to the Property Management module — first month free — to unlock units, leases, rent invoices, and M-Pesa collection.

Create a managed property, add units, invite tenants to the portal, and track who has paid — with exports for your accounts.

This is separate from public listings: one system for discovery, another for day-to-day operations.

### Chapter 10 — Team & close (7:15)

Under **Team**, invite colleagues by email. Approve them before they access the dashboard. Staff can manage listings and leads; owners control billing and invites.

**Messages** centralises inquiries so nothing slips through.

Explore **Analytics** for portfolio insights, and **Plan** when you need more units or team seats.

That's the NyumbaSearch property manager workflow — apply, dashboard, upload, manage, and grow.

Visit **nyumbasearch.com/manager** to get started. Questions? Email **nyumbasearch101@gmail.com**.
