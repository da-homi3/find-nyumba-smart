Let's add a listing. Open Properties, then Add property.

The wizard has four steps. First: Details.

Choose the property type — apartment, house, bedsitter, commercial, and more.

Add bedrooms, bathrooms, rent amount, and pricing mode — monthly, nightly, or sale.

Write a clear title and description. Select amenities tenants care about — parking, water, security, fibre.

Add contact name and phone numbers — tenants reach you directly through NyumbaSearch.

Step two: Photos and media.

Drag in high-quality photos — bright, honest, and well-lit. The platform enhances uploads for faster loading.

Add a walkthrough video or three-sixty tour if you have one. You can also paste a YouTube or Matterport link.

More media means more trust — and more viewing requests.

Step three: Map pin.

Drop the pin on the exact building location. Tenants use map search — accurate pins get found faster.

Pick the county and neighbourhood from Kenya location lists so filters work correctly.

Final step: Review.

Check every field, preview how the card will look, and run the listing quality check.

When you're satisfied, submit. New listings may go through verification before going live — that's how NyumbaSearch keeps the marketplace trustworthy.

You can edit, boost visibility, or deactivate any time from Properties.
