Welcome to NyumbaSearch for Property Managers.

This walkthrough uses the live website — nyumbasearch.com — and the real property manager dashboard.

From the homepage, open your portal. After approval, you land here: your dashboard.

This is your command centre. At the top: total properties, views, tenant leads, and potential monthly revenue.

The Tools row covers bulk import, API integrations, your plan, and billing.

Your properties lists live marketplace listings. Viewing requests appear on the right — confirm or decline in one click.

Use Add property any time you have a new unit to publish.

Open Properties to see every listing, its status, and quick actions.

Now upload a listing. The wizard has four steps: Details, Photos and media, Map pin, and Review.

Choose the property type. Add bedrooms, rent, a clear title, and contact details.

Then add photos — bright, honest, well-lit. Drop the map pin on the exact building so tenants can find you.

Review everything before you submit. New listings may go through verification. That's how NyumbaSearch keeps the marketplace trustworthy.

Tenants and rent is where you operate portfolios — units, leases, invoices, and M-Pesa collection.

Under Team, invite colleagues by email. Approve them before they can open this dashboard.

Analytics shows how your listings perform.

That's the live NyumbaSearch workflow: dashboard, upload properties, manage rent, and grow your team.

Visit nyumbasearch.com/manager to get started.
