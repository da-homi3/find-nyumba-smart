Welcome to NyumbaSearch for Property Managers.

This walkthrough uses the live website — nyumbasearch.com — so you see the same screens you'll use when you apply, list properties, and reach tenants.

Start on the homepage. NyumbaSearch is Nairobi's housing platform: verified owners, real listings, and tools for the people who manage them.

Open nyumbasearch.com/manager.

This is the Property Manager Portal. It's built for firms that onboard landlord clients, publish listings, and route tenant leads from one place.

Tap Create manager account.

Enter your organization name and contact details. NyumbaSearch operations reviews every application before dashboard access is granted. That review keeps the marketplace trustworthy.

Once approved, sign in here. After sign-in you land on your dashboard — properties, leads, viewing requests, team, billing, and Add property.

Your first paid month unlocks the portal. Your second month is free on manager plans.

Open nyumbasearch.com/pricing and scroll to Property Manager plans.

Solo Manager is for independent managers. Management Team is for growing firms — bulk import, more seats, more units. Enterprise is for large companies that need custom integrations.

After you upload a listing, this is what tenants see. Open Discover homes.

Filters, neighbourhoods, verification badges, and live inventory. Your listing competes here — so photos, price, and location need to be accurate.

Open any listing. This is the result of a successful upload: photos, rent, amenities, map, and a way for serious tenants to connect.

More honest media means more viewing requests.

Tenants also search on the map. Drop your pin on the exact building when you list — accurate pins get found faster.

Landlords can list directly too. Many managers work alongside owner accounts — same marketplace, different portal.

Questions about approval, plans, or onboarding? Use the contact page, or email nyumbasearch101@gmail.com.

That's the live NyumbaSearch path for property managers: apply at /manager, get approved, sign in to your dashboard, upload listings, and reach tenants on the same platform.

Visit nyumbasearch.com/manager to get started.
